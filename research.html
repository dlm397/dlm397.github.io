Current research and implementation · Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta

CLIFF: benefits cliffs and career advancement

The Career Ladder Identifier and Financial Forecaster (CLIFF) tools examine how benefits, taxes, earnings, and household expenses interact during career advancement, including situations where higher earnings trigger a loss of benefits. Working with Dr. David Altig, I help improve the experience of using and working with the tools, including the presentation of information and the workflows around it. Clearer presentation and more usable workflows are intended to support wider adoption and use of these research-informed tools.

Research assistant · Nyheim Plasma Institute · 2025–2026

Nonlinear electron transport in the magnetosphere

How do repeated interactions with whistler-mode waves spread electron energies and pitch angles? Working with Dr. Dmitri Vainchtein, I developed Julia simulations of resonant interactions and examined spreading across particle ensembles. In a separate observational strand, I built Python tools to organize and analyze ELFIN satellite electron spectra. The project page explains the methods and shows figures from the modeled interactions and spectral analysis.

Electron transport: Models, Satellite analysis, and Figures

Course research project · Drexel University · 2025

Common ownership and estimated airline productivity

This course research project examined the relationship between institutional investors' ownership of competing airlines and estimated airline productivity. I combined airline operational and financial data with institutional ownership data, estimated productivity, and used panel-data analysis to examine associations. The workflow drew on Department of Transportation Form 41 data and SEC Form 13F filings.

Independent exploration · Ongoing implementation

Probabilistic forecasting with Deadlock match data

I am using Deadlock match data as an ongoing implementation exercise alongside reading Simon Prince's Understanding Deep Learning. My proposed model combines spatial and temporal transformers, with two pre-training stages followed by win-prediction fine-tuning. The project page outlines the architecture and training plan; experiments and evaluation are still ahead.

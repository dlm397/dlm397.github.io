About me

I am a Research Analyst at the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta. My work includes policy-relevant macroeconomic research and the Career Ladder Identifier and Financial Forecaster (CLIFF) tools. I earned a B.S. in Economics and Mathematics from Drexel University in 2026.

My interests center on statistical inference, economic modeling, and computational methods for studying dynamic systems. I am particularly interested in how probabilistic models and numerical methods can help make sense of evolving economic and physical systems. My previous research at Drexel included particle simulations, satellite-data analysis, and computational economic modeling.

Research interests

  • Statistical Inference
  • Econometric & Structural Modeling
  • Numerical Methods for Dynamic Systems
  • Probabilistic Modeling & Stochastic Simulation

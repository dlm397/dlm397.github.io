Deadlock win prediction

I am using Deadlock match data as an implementation exercise alongside reading Simon Prince's Understanding Deep Learning. My goal is to predict win probabilities from match data while learning how architecture, optimization, and regularization choices effect a model. The outline describes my proposed approach, with experiments and evaluation happening currently.

Proposed architecture

I plan to combine hero embeddings and pre-match rank through a shared player encoder, then add current player features. A spatial transformer would model interactions within each frame. Pooling players by team and adding global features would produce frame tokens for a temporal transformer. A small prediction head would turn the observed match history into a win probability.

Three-stage training plan

  1. Draft pre-training: learn hero embeddings and a shared player encoder from hero selections, and pre-match ranks.
  2. Dynamics pre-training: transfer the encoder and train spatial and temporal attention on event-stream sequences, initially predicting next-frame changes in souls.
  3. Win-prediction fine-tuning: replace the dynamics head and fine-tune the full model on randomly sampled match prefixes, excluding terminal frames and outcome-revealing features.

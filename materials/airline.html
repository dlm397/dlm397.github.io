Common Ownership and Estimated Airline Productivity

Author
Dominic Marchese
Andrew Rogers
Claudia Adam
Course & period
Industrial Analysis
December · 2025
Tools
Stata & Python

This course research project examined the relationship between institutional investors' ownership of competing airlines and estimated airline productivity. We combined airline operational and financial data from the U.S. Department of Transportation Form 41 with institutional ownership data from SEC Form 13F filings to recover ownership, estimated total factor productivity via a revenue based LP production-function estimation, and used panel-data analysis to examine associations.

Career Ladder Identifier & Financial Forecaster

CLIFF is a suite of interactive tools that models how public benefits, taxes, tax credits, earnings, and household expenses interact as workers advance along a career path. The tools help individuals and advisers compare near-term choices, explore long-term career outcomes, and build individualized plans.

What is a benefits cliff?

A benefits cliff can occur when an increase in earnings triggers a loss of public assistance large enough to leave a household financially worse off. A benefits plateau occurs when the household is no better off after the change.

  • Make benefit changes visible before a transition
  • Compare career and training pathways
  • Support informed coaching conversations

What CLIFF brings together

The tools combine estimates of occupational wages, public-assistance receipt, taxes and tax credits, and basic household costs such as housing, childcare, health care, and transportation. This broader view helps make the financial consequences of career advancement easier to anticipate.

My Work on the Project

My role focuses on implementing user-friendly tools for data visualization, data storage, and data augmentation. This work supports clearer presentation and more effective use of the information that powers CLIFF's career and financial-planning experiences.

Designed for coaching

CLIFF is designed for use in coaching environments focused on economic mobility. Human-services agencies, workforce-development organizations, and financial coaches can use the tools to help clients understand tradeoffs and prepare for changes in public assistance.

Evidence from implementation

A two-year Atlanta Fed study examined CLIFF implementation at 23 organizations across 13 states. The findings emphasize identifying the appropriate users, integrating the tools into organizational workflows, and giving coaches enough time and training to discuss results effectively.
